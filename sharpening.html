our process

From Dull to Dangerous

Four steps. One perfect edge. Every knife that enters our studio follows the same ritual.

1

Inspection

We examine the edge under magnification to assess damage, angle, and the right approach.

2

Coarse Grind

We set the primary bevel on coarse stones (400–1000 grit), removing chips and reshaping the edge.

3

Refine & Polish

Progressive water stones from 3000 to 8000 grit refine the edge to a mirror-like polish.

4

Strop & Test

A leather strop removes the burr, and every blade is tested on paper and tomatoes before return.

common questions

You Asked, We Answer

How long does sharpening take?

Standard and Master services are typically ready within 48 hours. Full restorations may take 3–5 business days depending on the condition of the blade. Express touch-ups are same-day if dropped off before noon.

Do you sharpen serrated knives?

Yes, but we do it by hand with round files and ceramic rods — not on stones. Serrated sharpening starts at $35 per knife and typically takes 2–3 days.

Can you sharpen any brand or type of knife?

Absolutely. We sharpen Japanese, German, American, carbon steel, stainless, Damascus, and even ceramic blades (with a dedicated diamond stone). If it has an edge, we can improve it.

Do you offer mail-in sharpening?

Yes! Ship us your knives (we recommend a padded box with blade guards) and include a note with your name, contact info, and requested service. We'll sharpen, sanitize, and return them within 5 business days. Return shipping is a flat $12.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

We stand behind every edge we create. If you're not satisfied, bring it back and we'll re-sharpen it free of charge. If you're still not happy, we'll refund your service fee — no questions asked.

Ready to Feel the Difference?

A sharp knife changes everything. Bring us your dullest blade and we'll send you home with something that feels brand new.
