what drives us

Our Philosophy

Three principles guide every decision we make — from the steel we choose to the way we sharpen.

Honest Craft

No shortcuts, no compromises. Every blade is forged, heat-treated, and finished by hand — the same way it was done a century ago.

Lifetime Care

A knife should outlast its owner. We offer free sharpening for life and will repair any blade we've made — no questions asked.

Community First

We host workshops, sponsor local culinary students, and believe that great knives should be accessible to everyone who loves to cook.

our journey

Milestones Forged

From a one-man workshop to a beloved institution — here are the moments that shaped us.

1987 — The First Forge

Hiroshi Tanaka opens a small workshop in the Cutlery District, forging knives for local chefs. Word spreads fast.

1996 — The Sharpening Studio

We open our first sharpening service, using traditional Japanese whetstones. It quickly becomes the heart of our community.

2008 — Global Recognition

Our knives are featured in international culinary magazines. We begin shipping worldwide to chefs and home cooks alike.

Today — Still Forging

Three decades later, we still hammer every blade by hand. Our promise remains the same: honest craft, lifetime care, community first.
